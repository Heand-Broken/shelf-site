Shelf Pro

计划中，尚未开放购买 / Planned, not for sale yet

会包含什么

  • 全文索引：跨库倒排索引，搜索不再每次全表扫描，条目到几万条仍然瞬时返回。
  • 双链笔记导出：把剪藏导出成带双向链接的笔记，而不是一堆孤立文件。
  • 本地加密备份：导出加密包，密钥只在你手里，可以放进任意网盘而不担心被读。
所有 Pro 功能仍然跑在你自己的机器上。没有服务器，没有账号，没有订阅到期后锁住数据这回事。 买断的是功能，不是你的数据。

定价方向

一次性买断 预计 $9–15，最终以正式发布时为准。不做订阅，不做按月计费。

为什么还没开卖

商店版本本身还在审核流程里。在确认真的有人愿意买之前，我不会先去搭一套支付系统—— 那样做出来大概率是一个没人用的支付页面。

顺序是反过来的：先把免费版放出去，看有多少人真的在用、真的会因为缺全文索引而难受， 再决定要不要把 Pro 做出来，以及做到什么程度。这条路上有可能的结论是「Pro 不值得做」， 那也是一种结果。

Shelf Pro

Planned, not for sale yet

What it would include

  • A real full-text index — an inverted index across the whole library, so search stays instant instead of scanning every entry.
  • Linked-note export — clip pages into notes with backlinks rather than a folder of loose files.
  • Encrypted local backup — export an encrypted bundle; the key never leaves your machine, so any cloud drive will do.
Everything in Pro still runs on your own machine. No server, no account, and nothing that locks your data behind an expired subscription. You buy features, not your data.

Where the price is heading

One-time purchase Expected $9–15, final price set at launch. No subscription, no monthly billing.

Why it is not for sale yet

The free version is still going through store review. I am not going to build a payment system before there is any evidence that anyone would pay for it — the likely result of doing it in the other order is a checkout page nobody uses.

The order is: ship the free version, see how many people actually use it and how many actually need a full-text index, then decide whether Pro is worth building and how far to take it. "Pro is not worth building" is a possible outcome of that, and a fine one.
